📊 Model Specifications

  • Algorithm: Hyperparameter-Tuned Random Forest Classifier
  • Features Processed: 19 structural customer demographic and service metrics
  • Feature Transformations: Yeo-Johnson Transformation (to handle skewness) & Quantile Power Scalers
  • Scaling Pipeline: Integrated Robust Scaler Layer to reduce the influence of extreme outliers
  • Inference Engine: Probabilistic Decision Scoring evaluated via the predict_proba() matrix
  • Class Imbalance Management: Balanced using SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) to guarantee rigorous class sensitivity
  • Key Optimization Metrics: Optimized heavily for ROC-AUC and Recall scores to minimize false negatives in high-risk customer segments
  • Feature Importance Focus: Driven primarily by Tenure, Contract Type, and Monthly Charges as core predictors of customer retention lifecycles
